- `air.ts`、`weather.ts`、`data/schema/rules-constants.schema.ts`：按规则书交换两条规则的编号说明；
- `tests/engine/air-interaction.test.ts`、`tests/engine/rules-functions.test.ts`、`tests/flows/50-phase-e-reaction.test.ts`、`tests/flows/70-phase-g-exploitation.test.ts`：断言与标题改为 2.2d；
